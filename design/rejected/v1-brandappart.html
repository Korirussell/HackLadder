How it's scored

The full method, with the math, is public.

Who you beat matters

Winning against a room of strong hackers moves your rating more than winning a quiet weekend event.

One lucky win won't top it

Ratings start uncertain and settle as you compete, so a long record beats a single great weekend.

Every prize counts, not equally

Grand prizes count most, then runner-up, track and sponsor prizes. Submitting still counts.
